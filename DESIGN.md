# Puzzle desk

A restrained newspaper games-page treatment, not a reproduction of a publisher's brand. The visitor is solving a small puzzle on a phone or laptop, so the interface stays legible in daylight and does not hide the form behind a campaign-style hero.

- **Paper and ink:** oatmeal paper `#f8f6ef`, near-black ink `#242621`, brown-gray rules `#d5cfc2`. Dark green `#234c38` is for actions and links; guess tiles use distinct green, ochre and stone with accessible state names.
- **Type:** self-hosted Literata (Fontsource 5.3.0, SIL OFL; bundled notice in assets) for the masthead and headings, plain system sans on inputs, labels, actions and descriptions. Use tabular numerals for results and history.
- **Structure:** one ruled masthead, input and results columns, then links to daily play and guidance. On small screens put the form and its history first. Alignment and horizontal rules replace panels, shadows and gradients.
- **Signature:** the same five-column rhythm in the active clue and previous guesses. Minimal color changes show tile states; no decorative motion.
- **Copy:** no eyebrow labels, marketing copy, floating badges or mock newspaper slogans. Only instructions, states and clear links.
